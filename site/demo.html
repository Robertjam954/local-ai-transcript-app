Voice Summarizer · Demo
About GitHub

Try it - right here in your browser.

This demo runs the same idea as the full app, shrunk to fit a static page: OpenAI's Whisper (tiny.en) executes on your device via WebAssembly/WebGPU, and you can then search and ask questions across your transcripts with in-browser embeddings (local RAG). The full local app uses a larger Whisper model plus a local LLM for cleanup and retrieval.

🔒 Nothing is uploaded - audio and text stay on your device

1 · Capture

Record from your microphone or drop in an audio file (mp3, wav, webm, ogg, m4a). English only.

Model (~40 MB) downloads on first use, then is cached by your browser.

Raw transcript

2 · Optional: clean it up with an LLM (bring your own endpoint)

The request goes straight from your browser to the endpoint you specify - this site has no server and never sees your key. In the full app this step runs against a local model (Ollama / LM Studio), so no key is needed at all.

Cleaned transcript

3 · Ask your transcripts (local RAG)

Semantic search over your transcripts, running 100% in your browser (all-MiniLM embeddings via WebAssembly/WebGPU). Index some transcripts, then ask - every answer cites the passages it came from. Nothing is uploaded. Optionally add an LLM endpoint above to synthesize a written answer; otherwise the most relevant passages are shown.
