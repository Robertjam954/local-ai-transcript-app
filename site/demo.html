Try it - right here in your browser.

This demo runs the same idea as the full app, shrunk to fit a static page: OpenAI's Whisper (tiny.en) executes on your device via WebAssembly/WebGPU. The full local app uses a larger Whisper model plus a local LLM for cleanup.

🔒 Nothing is uploaded - audio and text stay on your device
